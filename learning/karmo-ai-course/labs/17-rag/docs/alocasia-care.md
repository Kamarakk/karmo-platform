# Variegated Alocasia care (sample)

Give bright, indirect light, about 1,500 to 3,000 lux. Direct sun burns the white sections.

Water when the top inch of the mix is dry. Soggy mix rots the corm.

Keep humidity at 60 to 80 percent and temperature between 65F and 85F.

Variegated leaves have less chlorophyll, so they grow slower than green ones. That is normal.
