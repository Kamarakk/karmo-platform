# Shipping policy (sample, replace with your real one)

We ship Monday through Wednesday only, so plants never sit in a warehouse over a weekend.

Heat packs are added when the overnight low on the route is below 50F. Cold packs are never used.

Plants ship bare-root in damp sphagnum moss, wrapped, and boxed with the leaves supported.

Orders over $150 ship Priority Mail Express at no extra cost.
